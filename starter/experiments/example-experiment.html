Research programs

Experiment title

Planned

In short

The claim, in the one sentence this experiment should let us say, with the prior work behind it. What we do, in two sentences.

What we do

The model, prompts, conditions and the one metric for each part of the claim, each defined so it cannot be read two ways after the results. What is intervened on, what stays fixed, where the outcome is measured. A diagram where it helps.

Competing explanations

Each explanation with its reference and what it predicts, chosen so that they predict different things. Then the other reasons each result could arise, and for each what in the design rules it out, or a plain statement that nothing does.

What we expect

The result each explanation predicts, with the thresholds decided now. A sketch of an expected result is labelled as a sketch, never drawn as data.

What would make us distrust a result

Checks decided in advance: what would mean the setup, not the claim, produced the number.

Results

Empty until the experiment has run. Run one case first and have a person read it before the rest. Then each number beside its control, with how many seeds it rests on.
